A real pass on a 32s talking-head clip, everything up to the paid generation. Proves the pipeline; the Omni submit is the only step that spends and waits for your go.

## 1. analyze-video (real output)

```json

```

## 2. craft-prompt (real, using recipes/holograms-vfx.md)
type = vfx → Omni two-part prompt. The subject is on screen, so PRESERVE names face + lip-sync:

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man, his face, his exact mouth movements and timing, his
clothing, his position and framing, the room, and the camera. Keep the subject and
performance identical to the source, frame for frame.
CHANGE (one effect): a glowing translucent holographic node-graph interface appears floating
in the air in front of him — softly pulsing cyan nodes connected by thin light lines — casting
a faint cyan glow onto his face and hands; it drifts and reacts subtly to his gestures.
```

## 3. assemble (cut) — proven
```bash
ffmpeg -i input/clip.mp4 -ss 17 -to 21 -c:v libx264 -r 30 -g 30 -keyint_min 30 \
  -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart assets/beat-04/src.mp4
```
(Verified on a real 1920×1080 clip → clean 4s h264+aac, aspect auto-detected 16:9.)

## 4. omni-vfx (STAGED — ready to submit, awaiting your go)
`assets/beat-04/omni.json` — the exact body. Only `video_list[0].url` needs the hosted `src.mp4`:
```json
{ "model": "gemini-omni-video",
  "input": {
    "prompt": "PRESERVE (do not change): the man, his face, his exact mouth movements and timing, his clothing, his position and framing, the room, and the camera. Keep the subject and performance identical to the source, frame for frame. CHANGE (one effect): a glowing translucent holographic node-graph interface appears floating in the air in front of him — softly pulsing cyan nodes connected by thin light lines — casting a faint cyan glow onto his face and hands; it drifts and reacts subtly to his gestures.",
    "video_list": [{ "url": "<hosted src.mp4>", "start": 0, "ends": 4 }],
    "aspect_ratio": "16:9",
    "resolution": "1080p" } }
```
Run: `kie.sh upload assets/beat-04/src.mp4` → paste the URL → `kie.sh submit assets/beat-04/omni.json` → `wait`. **This is the paid step — needs a go.**
