# Recipe — physics hook (impossible physics, scroll-stopper)

**Effect:** liquid defies gravity, an object floats/shatters, a surface ripples.
**Film:** perform the real action (tilt an empty glass, hold out an object). ≤10s.

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man, his hand and grip, the glass's shape and motion, the
background, and the camera. Keep everything not named below identical to the source.
CHANGE (one effect): [blue water] fills the [glass] and, as he tilts it upside down, the
water clings inside and refuses to fall — a smooth cohesive blob defying gravity, with
correct refraction and highlights through the glass.
```
**Known failure:** simple logic checks (asking for full levitation of a person) often return the source unchanged — physics *modifiers* on a held object work far better than whole-body defiance. Keep the effect local to the prop.
