# Recipe — self restyle (stylize the subject, keep lip-sync)

**Effect:** real you → claymation / anime / 3D-render version, mouth still in sync.
**Film:** clean talking-head, even light, plain-ish background. ≤10s.

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man's exact mouth movements and timing, his head pose and
motion, his framing, and the camera. The performance and lip-sync must match the source
frame for frame.
CHANGE (one effect): render the entire shot in [claymation stop-motion style] — he becomes
a [clay figure] with visible fingerprint texture and soft studio light, and the background
matches the same style.
```
**Known failure:** style + a second change (wardrobe, set) at once = total drift. Style only. If lip-sync slips, strengthen the PRESERVE timing line.
