# Recipe — logo / artwork wrap (2D art onto a moving 3D surface)

**Effect:** your logo or artwork tracks onto a card, laptop lid, box, or shirt you move.
**Film:** slowly turn/tilt the blank surface toward camera. Keep rotation partial (< ~180°). ≤10s.
**Refs:** the flat artwork as a clean image (front — and back only if it flips).

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man's hand, the object's shape and motion, the background,
and the lighting. Keep everything not named below identical to the source.
CHANGE (one effect): apply [Image 1] as the printed artwork on the front face of the [card]
he is turning; it tracks the surface with correct perspective, wrapping and lighting as it
rotates.
```
**Known failure (documented):** past ~180° rotation the model may repeat the front art on the back — keep the turn partial, or generate front and back as two beats. Fine text on the art garbles → keep art graphic-heavy.
