# Recipe — product swap

**Effect:** a blank/plain object in hand becomes a branded product (can, box, bottle, cup).
**Film:** hold the plain object and rotate it slowly, good light, object fills frame. ≤10s.
**Refs (optional):** a flat image of the target label/product (≤7).

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man's face, his exact mouth movements and timing, his hand
and skin, the background, and the existing lighting. Keep everything not named below
identical to the source.
CHANGE (one effect): the plain [can] in his hand becomes a [brushed-aluminium energy drink]
with [condensation drops]; the label wraps correctly around the curve and its reflections
and highlights track the object as he rotates it under the existing key light.
```
**Known failure:** small label text garbles → keep label art bold/simple, or add the fine text as a `graphic` overlay after. Fast rotation loses tracking → rotate slowly.
