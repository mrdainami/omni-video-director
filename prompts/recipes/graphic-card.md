# Recipe — graphic card (GPT-Image-2)

**Use for:** stat cards, 3-point lists, titles, quote cards, logo lockups — anything with real text.
**Refs:** logos as real transparent PNGs; any product/brand imagery. ≤16.

```
=== REFERENCE ROLE MAPPING ===
- [Image 1]: the product/brand logo — use exactly, keep shape + colour, do not redraw
=== COMPOSITION & BLOCKING ===
Centered card on a clean background; logo top-left; three stat rows stacked centre.
=== ENVIRONMENT & STYLE ===
Dark editorial background #0E1418; one accent colour #FF6A45; generous whitespace; modern sans.
=== TEXT RENDERING CONSTRAINTS ===
Render exactly, verbatim: "3,000 HRS SAVED" (large, white, centre), "0 EXTRA HIRES" (accent),
"1 SYSTEM" (white). No other text.
=== FINAL QUALITY DIRECTIVES ===
Crisp vector-like text, high contrast, use the reference logo exactly. Match the video aspect.
```
**Rules:** quote every word verbatim; logos as images, never drawn; set aspect = the input video's aspect; `2K` default. Transient kie `500` → resubmit (0 credits on fail).
