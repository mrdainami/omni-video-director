# Recipe — set transform (background change, keep the subject)

**Effect:** your room becomes a new set (neon studio, office, outdoor) while you + lip-sync stay.
**Film:** any talking-head clip works — no special shoot. ≤10s window.

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man, his face, his exact mouth movements and timing, his
clothing, his position and scale in frame, and the camera framing. Keep the subject fully
identical to the source.
CHANGE (one effect): replace only the background environment behind him with a [dark neon
cyberpunk studio with soft magenta and cyan rim light]; the new lighting spills believably
onto his shoulders and hair edges.
```
**Known failure:** if PRESERVE omits "position and scale", he drifts/rescales. If the new light is extreme, the face relights oddly — keep spill "soft/subtle".
