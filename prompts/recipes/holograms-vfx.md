# Recipe — holograms / particles (add VFX to empty space)

**Effect:** floating UI panels, data particles, or objects appear around you as you gesture.
**Film:** gesture at empty air where the element should be (cup your hand, point). ≤10s.

```
PRESERVE (do not change): the man, his face, his exact mouth movements and timing, his
hands and gestures, the room, and the camera. Keep the subject and environment identical.
CHANGE (one effect): a [glowing translucent holographic UI panel with faint data readouts]
appears floating above his open palm, casting a soft cyan glow onto his hand; it drifts and
flickers subtly with his movement.
```
**Known failure:** asking for text on the hologram → it garbles; keep the panel abstract and add real labels as a `graphic` overlay. Multiple elements at once can fight — one element per generation.
